Our Mission

Fighting HIV/AIDS
With Compassion

Apaana Foundation was born from a belief that no one should face HIV/AIDS alone. We provide comprehensive care — from testing to treatment to emotional support.

🔬
Prevention & Testing Free HIV testing, counseling, and awareness campaigns across 47 communities
💊
Treatment Support ART medication access, adherence counseling, and nutritional support programs
🤝
Community Empowerment Reducing stigma and building resilient communities through education and peer support
👶
PMTCT Programs Preventing mother-to-child transmission — protecting the next generation
