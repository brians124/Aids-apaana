Who We Are

A Foundation Built
on Compassion &
Community

Apaana AIDS Foundation was founded in 2016 by a group of Ugandan health workers, community leaders, and people living with HIV who refused to accept silence and stigma. We operate at the intersection of healthcare, education, and human dignity.

Our holistic approach addresses not just the medical needs of those living with HIV/AIDS, but their emotional, social, and economic wellbeing — because true health is whole health.

Youth HIV Education
School-based awareness programs equipping young people with knowledge, skills, and confidence to protect themselves and others.
Reaching 3,200+ youth

Maternal
PMTCT — Baby Shield
Preventing mother-to-child HIV transmission through prenatal care, testing, counseling, and medication for expectant mothers.
890+ babies born HIV-free

Free Testing & Counseling
Mobile HIV testing units and fixed counseling centres offering confidential, free testing and immediate linkage to care.
18,000+ tests done

Stigma Reduction
Community dialogues, peer support groups, and media campaigns that break down the stigma and discrimination faced by those living with HIV.
47 communities engaged
