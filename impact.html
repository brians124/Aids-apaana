Success Stories

Lives Changed by Your Support

Behind every statistic is a human story. Here are the voices of those whose lives have been transformed.

Sarah N.

Mother of 3 · Kampala District
The Challenge

Diagnosed HIV-positive in 2021, Sarah faced stigma, lack of medication access, and family rejection in her rural community.

How We Helped

Through our treatment support program, Sarah received ART guidance, regular home visits, and peer group enrollment.

"I was about to give up. AIDS APAANA gave me a community that understands me. My children have their mother back."

James O.

Youth Peer Educator · Jinja
The Challenge

At 19, James had no knowledge about HIV prevention and was engaging in risky behaviors without any education or support.

How We Helped

Enrolled in our youth training program, James became a certified peer educator reaching over 200 young people in his community.

"I now teach others what saved my own life. Knowledge is the most powerful prevention tool we have."

Grace M.

Small Business Owner · Masaka
The Challenge

A widow living with HIV, Grace struggled to feed her children and afford ARTs while facing deep community stigma.

How We Helped

Through our women's economic empowerment program, Grace received business skills training and a startup grant. She now runs a successful tailoring business.

"AIDS APAANA didn't just help me survive HIV — they helped me build a future for my family."

Peter K.

Community Health Worker · Luwero
The Challenge

Peter's entire village had zero access to HIV testing, with myths and stigma preventing anyone from seeking help.

How We Helped

AIDS APAANA established a community testing drive reaching 340 people, training Peter as a village health volunteer.

"We tested 340 people in three days. Some cried because they finally knew their status. That changed everything."

Financial Transparency

Your Trust, Our Accountability

We are committed to the highest standards of financial transparency and accountability.

How Funds Are Used

75%

AIDS APAANA is committed to ensuring that the maximum proportion of every donation goes directly to our programs and beneficiaries. We maintain strict financial controls and publish annual audited reports.

  • Annual independent external financial audit
  • Quarterly financial reporting to our board
  • Public disclosure of all donor-funded programs
  • Zero-tolerance policy on financial misconduct
  • Registered with Uganda NGO Bureau
  • Compliant with International Financial Reporting Standards

Recent Achievements

Our Journey of Impact

A chronicle of milestones that mark our growing footprint across Uganda.

2025
🌟 Expansion

Reached 3,000 Youth

Successfully completed awareness campaigns reaching over 3,000 young people across 8 districts.

📍 Growth

4 New Communities

Expanded operations into 4 new communities in Northern Uganda, increasing our geographic footprint significantly.

💊 Health

95% Adherence Milestone

Achieved a landmark 95% ART adherence rate among all supported clients — exceeding our annual target.

🤝 Partnership

Ministry of Health MOU

Signed a memorandum of understanding with the Ministry of Health to integrate our programs into national HIV response.

2024
🏠 Support

800 Families Supported

Provided comprehensive household support packages including nutritional aid, counseling, and treatment linkages to 800 families.

💻 Innovation

Digital Education Platform

Launched an innovative digital HIV education platform, making resources accessible to youth via mobile phones.

🎓 Training

250 Peer Educators Certified

Completed a rigorous peer educator training program with 250 young people certified to deliver HIV education.

📊 Audit

Clean Audit Certification

Received a clean audit certification from our independent external auditors for the third consecutive year.
