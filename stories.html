Respectful Storytelling

How we tell stories

People living with or affected by HIV/AIDS often face stigma. These guidelines protect the people who trust us with their stories.

Consent first

No story is published without the storyteller's clear, written consent. They can withdraw it at any time.

Their words, their choice

Storytellers decide what to share, how they are named, and whether to use a photo. Anyone may stay anonymous.

Privacy and safety

We never publish health details, locations or names of other people without their permission.

Dignity, not pity

We show people as whole individuals with strengths and goals, not only as people in need.

Truth only

Stories are never invented, exaggerated or combined. Sample stories are always labelled as placeholders.

Care for children

Stories involving anyone under 18 need a parent or guardian's consent and extra care with identity.

Share Your Story

Your story, your terms

You can share with your name or anonymously. You decide what to include. We will contact you before anything is published.

  • Write your story, or a short note saying you would like to share.
  • Choose whether to use your name or stay anonymous.
  • Our team replies to confirm what can be published and how.
  • You can ask us to change or remove your story at any time.
