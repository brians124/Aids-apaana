A Message from the Executive Director

AO Executive Director

Every life matters. At AIDS APAANA, we believe that access to care, knowledge, and dignity is a fundamental human right — not a privilege. Over the past 15 years, I have witnessed the transformation that happens when communities are empowered with information, compassion, and the right resources.

Our leadership team is united by one unwavering commitment: to leave no one behind in the fight against HIV/AIDS. Together, we are not just managing a crisis — we are building a future where the next generation grows up free from this disease and its stigma.

Our Journey

Leadership Timeline

From humble beginnings to a nationally recognized force in HIV/AIDS advocacy.

2010
Organization founded with a core team of 5 health advocates committed to ending stigma in local communities.
2013
First regional health clinic established, offering free HIV testing and counseling to 3,000+ individuals annually.
2015
Expanded outreach to rural areas with mobile health units, reaching underserved communities across 5 provinces.
2018
Launched partnership with WHO and UNAIDS to implement a national peer-education youth program.
2020
Led National HIV Awareness Campaign reaching 10 million people through media, schools, and community events.
2025
Launched Digital Health Transformation initiative, integrating telemedicine and AI-assisted diagnostics into our programs.
