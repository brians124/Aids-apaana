Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible? +
Yes. AIDS APAANA is a registered nonprofit NGO. All donations are eligible for tax deductions. You will receive a receipt by email after your donation.
How are funds used? +
45% goes to direct patient support, 25% to HIV awareness campaigns, 20% to medication access programs, and only 10% to operational costs. We publish annual impact reports for full transparency.
Can I make a monthly recurring donation? +
Absolutely! Simply select "Monthly" on the donation form. Monthly supporters receive exclusive impact updates and a special donor recognition certificate.
Is my payment information secure? +
Yes. All transactions are encrypted with 256-bit SSL security. We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and mobile money. We never store your card details.
How do I volunteer with AIDS APAANA? +
Visit our volunteer page or email us at volunteer@aidsapaana.org. We welcome medical professionals, educators, counsellors, and community advocates.
