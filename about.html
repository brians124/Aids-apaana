Who We Are

Fighting HIV/AIDS with Compassion

AIDS APAANA Foundation is a community-driven non-profit organization working tirelessly to reduce stigma, provide support, and empower those most affected by HIV/AIDS across multiple districts.

We believe every person deserves dignity, access to healthcare, and a community that cares. Our work spans awareness campaigns, direct patient support, counseling services, and advocacy for policy change.

We help individuals living with HIV/AIDS, their families, orphaned children, sex workers, and other vulnerable populations who face compounded social and health challenges.

Our Journey

How We Grew

From a small grassroots idea to a recognised foundation — here are the milestones that define our story.

2023
Foundation Established
AIDS APAANA Foundation was officially registered as an NGO, with a vision to serve communities affected by HIV/AIDS.
2024
First Community Outreach
Launched our first health camps, awareness drives, and counseling sessions reaching over 500 individuals across 3 districts.
2024 Q4
Medical Partnership
Established partnerships with local clinics and health departments to provide free ART (antiretroviral therapy) support.
2025
Multi-District Expansion
Expanded operations to 12+ districts, launched a scholarship program for children affected by HIV/AIDS, and reached 2,500+ beneficiaries.
2026
Growing Impact
Continuing to scale operations, strengthen community networks, and advocate for policy changes at district and state levels.
Core Beliefs

Our Mission, Vision & Values

🎯

Our Mission

To reduce HIV/AIDS-related stigma, provide holistic support to affected individuals and families, and promote awareness, prevention, and dignified access to care across communities.

🔭

Our Vision

A world where every person living with HIV/AIDS is treated with dignity, has access to quality healthcare, and is supported by a compassionate, informed community.

💠

Our Values

  • Compassion for every individual
  • Dignity and non-discrimination
  • Community-first approach
  • Transparency & accountability
  • Inclusive, evidence-based care
What We Do

Our Key Programs

Each program is designed to address a specific need — from prevention to long-term care and social support.

🏥

Medical Support

Free ART assistance, regular health check-ups, and referrals to government hospitals for people living with HIV/AIDS.

📢

Awareness & Prevention

Community campaigns, school sensitisation drives, and workshops to educate people about HIV/AIDS prevention and reduce stigma.

🧠

Counseling Services

One-on-one and group counseling sessions by trained professionals for patients and their caregivers.

🎓

Children's Scholarship

Educational sponsorship for children orphaned or affected by HIV/AIDS, helping break the cycle of poverty and exclusion.

💼

Livelihood Training

Vocational skills and micro-enterprise support for affected women and youth to become financially independent.

🤝

Community Networks

Building peer-support groups and community networks so no one faces HIV/AIDS alone.

FAQs

Common Questions

Everything you might want to know about AIDS APAANA Foundation.

Yes, AIDS APAANA Foundation is registered as a non-governmental organisation (NGO) under the Societies Registration Act. Our registration certificate and legal documents are available upon request. We operate with full legal compliance and transparency.
Donations are used directly for program delivery — including medical support, counseling, awareness campaigns, and scholarships. We keep administrative costs minimal. A detailed breakdown of fund usage is published in our annual report. You can also request an expenditure summary at any time.
We welcome volunteers from all backgrounds — healthcare professionals, students, social workers, and community advocates. You can apply through our website or contact us directly. We offer orientation training and connect you with the most suitable program for your skills.
We serve individuals living with HIV/AIDS, their families and caregivers, children orphaned by AIDS, sex workers, and other vulnerable groups facing health and social challenges. Our programs are free of charge for all beneficiaries.
Yes! We accept in-kind donations such as medicines, nutritional supplements, stationery for children, and household essentials. Please contact us first so we can guide you on what is needed most and arrange collection or drop-off.
