Your Donation In Action

Every Shilling Makes
a Real Difference

Here's exactly what your contribution provides for people living with HIV/AIDS in Uganda.

🧪
UGX 10,000
Provides HIV rapid testing kits for two people, enabling early detection and treatment.
💊
UGX 20,000
Covers one month of nutritional supplements for a child on ARV therapy.
🧠
UGX 50,000
Funds a full counselling session for a person newly diagnosed with HIV.
👨‍👩‍👧
UGX 100,000
Provides a family with one month of care supplies, including hygiene and medical essentials.
🏫
UGX 250,000
Sponsors school fees for one orphan or vulnerable child for an entire school term.
🌍
UGX 500,000
Funds a community outreach event that reaches 150+ people with HIV education and testing.
