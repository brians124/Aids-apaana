FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

You can register as a volunteer by emailing volunteer@aidsapaana.org or filling out the contact form above with "Volunteering" as your subject. We welcome community health workers, educators, counselors, and general helpers.

Donations can be made via bank transfer, mobile money, or online through our website. Contact donate@aidsapaana.org for account details and impact reports. We accept both one-time and monthly recurring donations.

Yes — all counseling services and communications with AIDS APAANA are completely confidential. We comply with Uganda's health privacy regulations. You can speak openly with our team without fear of disclosure.

Our head office is at Plot 14, Kampala Road, Kampala. We also operate outreach centers in Kawempe, Makindye, Wakiso, and Entebbe. See the map above for exact directions. Call us if you need specific transport guidance.

Yes. Free, confidential HIV testing and counseling is available at all our outreach centers. No appointment is necessary for most locations. Call our health team at health@aidsapaana.org for clinic schedules.

We aim to respond to all enquiries within 24 business hours. For urgent matters, please call our main line or WhatsApp us directly. Emergency health calls are answered 24/7 on our hotline.
