FAQ

Questions we're often asked

Can't find your answer here? Send us a message and we'll help.

What is the fastest way to reach you?
Call or message us on WhatsApp using the phone number on this page. For longer questions, email us or use the contact form.
How do I make a donation?
You can give through our donation page. If you have a question before giving, contact us and choose "Donations" as the subject.
How can I volunteer with AIDS APAANA?
Visit our volunteer page to apply. You can also message us with the subject "Volunteering" to ask what support is needed.
Can my organisation partner with or sponsor you?
Yes. Send us a message with the subject "Partnership or sponsorship" and tell us about your organisation and what you have in mind.
Is it safe to share personal health details in the form?
We recommend keeping the form to general information. If you need to discuss something personal or sensitive, contact us by phone or WhatsApp so we can speak with you directly.
Can I visit your office?
Please contact us first so we can confirm the location and a suitable time for your visit.
NEED HELP NOW?

If it's urgent, don't wait for a reply

This form and our email are not monitored as emergency services. In an urgent situation, get care first, then reach out to us for support.

Medical emergency

Go to the nearest hospital or health centre straight away, or ask someone nearby to take you.

Possible recent HIV exposure

Post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP) works best when started as soon as possible, and no later than 72 hours after exposure. Go to a health facility immediately.

Urgent support from our team

Call or WhatsApp us and tell us it's urgent. We'll do our best to respond and point you to the right help.
