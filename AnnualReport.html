Executive Summary

A Year of Transformation

Reflecting on milestones achieved, communities served, and goals that continue to inspire our mission forward.

This year marked a defining chapter for ADIS APAANA. We expanded our reach to new districts, deepened our partnerships, and most importantly — we witnessed real, tangible change in the lives of thousands. Our commitment to transparent governance and community-first programming remains the cornerstone of everything we do.

Dr. Amara Diallo
Executive Director, ADIS APAANA

Impact Stories

Change We Created Together

Beyond the numbers — the human stories that define who we are and why we do what we do.

Water

Community Water Initiative

Provided clean, safe water access to over 3,000 families across 6 rural communities, reducing waterborne disease by 67%.

3,000+ families impacted

Girls' Education Scholarship

Awarded 480 scholarships to young women in underserved communities, with a 94% retention rate throughout the academic year.

Mobile Health Clinics

Year in Review

2025 Milestones

Key moments and achievements across the year.

January 2025

Strategic Plan Launch

Launched our 3-year strategic framework focusing on health, education, and sustainable livelihoods with community co-design.

March 2025

Water Project Phase II Completion

Successfully completed Phase II of the Community Water Initiative, connecting 12 additional villages to clean water infrastructure.

May 2025

International Partnership Signed

Formalized a 3-year partnership with the Global Health Foundation valued at $850,000 for mobile health expansion.

August 2025

Girls' Scholarship Programme Launch

Opened applications for our expanded scholarship programme with 480 awards — a 60% increase from the previous cycle.

October 2025

Annual Community Summit

Hosted 600+ community leaders, donors, and partners for our largest convening — resulting in 8 new collaboration agreements.

December 2025

Year-End Audit & Report

Completed independent financial audit with clean opinion. Funds utilized at 96.4% efficiency — a new organizational record.
