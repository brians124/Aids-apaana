    What we ask for, why we ask, and how to stop emails at any time.

    Privacy information

    We keep this simple. Here is how your details are used for the newsletter.

    • What we collect. Your name, your email address, and the topics and frequency you choose. Nothing else is required.
    • Why. To send you the newsletter you asked for and to remember your preferences.
    • Who sees it. We do not sell or rent your details. Once sign-up is connected, they will be held by the email service we use to send the newsletter, and we will name that service here.
    • Your rights. You can ask us to show, correct or delete what we hold about you by emailing info@apaana.org.
    • More detail. Read our full Privacy Policy.

    Unsubscribe

    You can leave the list whenever you like, with no explanation needed.

    1. Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any newsletter email once we are sending them.
    2. Or email us from the address you subscribed with and we will remove you.
    3. Want fewer emails instead? Ask us to change your topics or frequency.
