FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my payment secure?
Yes, absolutely. All transactions are encrypted with 256-bit SSL technology. We partner with trusted payment processors including Flutterwave, PayPal, and Stripe, which are PCI-DSS compliant. Your financial information is never stored on our servers.
Can I donate anonymously?
Yes! Simply tick the "Donate anonymously" checkbox on the donation form. Your name will not appear on the public donor wall, but you will still receive an email receipt for your records.
How are donations used?
65% of all donations go directly to beneficiary programs. 15% supports medical and health services, 12% funds education and scholarships, 5% covers operational costs, and 3% is held in an emergency reserve. We publish quarterly financial reports on our website.
Will I receive a receipt for my donation?
Yes, a donation receipt is sent immediately to your email address after a successful transaction. The receipt includes your name, amount, transaction ID, and a PDF download option for your records.
Can I cancel a recurring donation?
Yes, you can cancel your recurring donation at any time by contacting us via email at donations@hopefoundation.ug or calling +256 700 123 456. We will process the cancellation within 24 hours with no questions asked.
Which countries can donate?
We accept donations from worldwide. You can donate using international payment methods including PayPal, credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), Stripe, and Flutterwave which supports payments from 150+ countries in multiple currencies.
Is Hope Foundation a registered NGO?
Yes, Hope Foundation Uganda is a fully registered non-governmental organization with the Uganda NGO Bureau (Registration No. NGO/12345/2018). We are also registered with the Uganda Revenue Authority and comply with all national regulations.
